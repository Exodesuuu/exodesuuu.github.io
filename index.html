Frequently Asked Questions

Answers & Inquiries

Everything you need to know about commissioning custom sportswear designs, sublimation vector files, and turnaround times.

What services does James Mesa specialize in?

I specialize in full-sublimation sportswear graphics (basketball jerseys, baseball uniforms, football kits, polo shirts, hoodies), realistic 3D mockup presentations, factory-ready vector pattern preparations, and athletic team logo branding.

What file formats do I receive for manufacturing?

You receive 100% vector print-ready source files (Adobe Illustrator .AI, EPS, and high-resolution PDF) configured with precise CMYK color modes for sublimation printers, plus high-definition PNG/JPG 3D mockups.

How much does a custom design commission cost?

Single pre-made design licenses from my catalog start at $49 USD. Custom commissions built from scratch and bulk team packages (with full player rosters) are quoted based on specific design requirements and team size.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, I collaborate with sports teams, apparel manufacturers, and clothing lines worldwide (US, Australia, UK, Canada, and Asia). Accepted payment methods include PayPal, Wise, Bank Transfer, GCash, and PayMaya.

Do you lay out full team rosters with names & numbers?

Yes! The Bulk Team Package includes complete typography layouts with individualized player names and numbers fitted across all garment sizes from youth to adult 3XL+ ready for your garment printer.

What is the standard turnaround time?

Initial 3D mockup concepts are typically delivered within 24 to 48 business hours. Revisions are completed quickly to ensure you meet your factory production deadlines.
